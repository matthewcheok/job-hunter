The team combines hands-on AI implementation with full-stack delivery — covering frontend, backend, infrastructure, and LLM integration in-house, without external subcontracting.

**Founders**

Rakuda AI was founded by two serial entrepreneurs with complementary backgrounds in product, commerce, and AI.

**Masaki Arakawa — Representative Director (CEO equivalent)**

A serial entrepreneur with a track record of building and exiting a software-services company. Arakawa graduated from Keio University and then founded Amy Inc., which built a custom-software-development and marketing-support business. Arakawa successfully exited the company through a sale to Vector Inc., a Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime-listed group. At Rakuda AI, Arakawa leads overall strategy, client engagement, and delivery oversight.

**Yudai Futai — Co-founder**

A serial operator with deep experience across e-commerce, IoT, and HR-tech. Futai graduated from Kyoto University’s Faculty of Economics. He began his career at Rakuten as an e-commerce consultant, then moved to the IoT startup Qrio Inc. as Manager of Business Development. He went on to co-found scouty (now LAPRAS Inc.), a Japanese AI-driven HR-tech company. At Rakuda AI, Futai leads product, partnerships, and AI-product strategy.

**What We Do**

Rakuda AI specializes in shipping production-grade AI products on aggressive timelines. Typical engagements include:

- **AI-integrated web applications** — LLM/RAG-based search, document analysis, conversational interfaces
- **E-commerce builds** — Marketplaces, custom storefronts, payment & inventory integration
- **Internal business systems** — Dashboards, workflow automation, data pipelines
- **AI feature embedding** — Adding natural-language search, summarization, and automation into existing SaaS

**Strengths**

- **AI Implementation** — Multiple LLM / generative-AI products in production, owned end-to-end
- **Full-Stack Delivery** — Frontend, backend, infrastructure, and AI integration all handled in-house
- **Speed** — AI-assisted development workflows deliver projects 2–3× faster than industry standard
- **E-commerce Track Record** — Marketplace platforms shipped in as little as one month
